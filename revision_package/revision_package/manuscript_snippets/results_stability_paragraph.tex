Error rates fell with label agreement: 67.7\% (42/62) below 0.5, 33.8\% (24/71) for 0.5--0.79, 16.1\% (10/62) for 0.8--0.99, and 0\% (0/62) when all retrieved documents agreed with the prediction. As an error-ranking score, one minus label agreement had an AUROC of 0.844 (95\% CI 0.796--0.888), comparable to the existing selector scores (0.817--0.833, overlapping intervals); we do not claim a difference. Truncating the retrieved set to 3, 5 or 7 documents changed the majority label in 12.8\%, 7.0\% and 5.1\% of records, respectively.
